Shadow Batter 🏏
A mixed reality cricket training tool for Meta Quest 3.

Shadow Batter is a mental rehearsal and neurological training tool designed for competitive batsmen. It uses mixed reality to overlay a virtual bowler onto the real world — so players can practice reading deliveries, making decisions, and rehearsing shots with a real bat in hand, without needing a physical bowler or full net setup.

This is a training tool, not a video game. The goal is to sharpen a batter's mental preparation, shot selection, and decision-making under realistic conditions.
What it does
MR Passthrough: The real world stays visible. The virtual bowler is overlaid on top. You swing a real bat in a real space.
Configurable Bowler: Speed, swing, seam, line, length, bowler type, arm angle — all adjustable to simulate specific match scenarios.
Spatial Anchoring: Virtual stumps and crease lines are locked to your physical floor, so the batting environment feels real and grounded.
Dynamic Ball Physics: Ball trajectory responds to bowling parameters in real time — no pre-baked animations.
Why I built this
I'm a professional cricketer (1st Division league, Bengaluru) and a CSE student. I built Shadow Batter because I wanted a tool that lets batsmen prepare mentally for specific bowlers and situations — the kind of preparation that currently only exists in your head or in expensive team setups. MR makes it possible to do this alone, anywhere, with a bat in hand.
